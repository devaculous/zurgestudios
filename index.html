The Studio

Built
Different. On purpose.

Erbium Interactive was founded in 2026 by Devaculous with one obsession: make games that are genuinely, deeply fun and realistic — and back that up with lore that actually feels good.

"We make money so we can keep crafting games — not the other way around."

No gamepass milking. No repeating tasks you can skip with a purchase. No locking the best content behind a paywall. We're here to make worlds worth losing yourself in.
