Project Revenant

A decorated Marine, paralyzed in the line of duty, is quietly recruited by a shadow branch of the CIA. Restored with an experimental neural AI bridge and advanced combat armor, he becomes one of the agency's most capable field agents — driven by one hope: to return home to the family he almost lost forever.
