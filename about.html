The Manifesto

Make It
Matter.

Erbium Interactive exists because we think players deserve better than an endless treadmill of monetisation, recycled mechanics and worlds with nothing to say.

We're building games in Roblox because the platform gives us a place to experiment, reach players quickly and create worlds that can grow with their communities.

"We make money so we can keep crafting games — not the other way around."

That philosophy shapes everything: how we design progression, how we approach lore, how we decide what belongs behind a purchase, and how we listen when players tell us something isn't working.

We're a small studio. That means every system, story beat and strange little detail has a reason to exist.

01

Player First

Your time matters. Your attention matters. We want players to feel rewarded for playing, not pressured into paying.

02

Worlds With Weight

We want lore to feel discoverable and connected. The environment, mechanics and story should speak the same language.

03

Independent By Choice

Staying small lets us move deliberately, take creative risks and keep the work close to the people making it.
