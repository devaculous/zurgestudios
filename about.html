The
Manifesto. Read it.

Erbium Interactive was founded in 2026 by Devaculous. Not because the market needed another studio — but because it needed a different kind of studio.

We started with a simple question: why aren't more Roblox games just… fun? Not designed to keep you playing. Designed to make you actually want to.

"We don't make games to keep you playing. We make games you'll actually want to play."

The industry has a problem: the people making financial decisions have never played the games they fund. We're the opposite. Every decision at Erbium starts with one question: is this better for the player?

We're not stretching a game to inflate session time, or hiding the best parts behind a paywall. If you spend money on something we've made, it should be because you love the game — not because the free version is frustrating by design.

We're also obsessive about story. Not lore dumps and wikis. Real, breathing narrative embedded in the world itself — the kind where you're reading item descriptions at 2am because the world is just that interesting.

  • 01

    Fun Comes First

    Games should be fun because they're fun — not because we've stacked a hundred engagement systems. If something isn't enjoyable, we change it. Simple as that.

  • 02

    Story Should Matter

    We want stories that are actually part of the world — the kind you notice while playing and keep thinking about after you log off.

  • 03

    Respect the Player

    You can tell when a game is trying to waste your time or push you into spending. We don't do that. If we wouldn't want to experience it, we won't put it in our game.

  • 04

    Make It Worth Playing

    We'd rather take our time and ship something we're proud of than rush it out. Games don't need to be perfect — but they should feel like we actually cared.
